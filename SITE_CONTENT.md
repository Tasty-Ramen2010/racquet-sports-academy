# Design Vision (from hand sketch IMG_4470)

1. **Loading screen** — interactive 3D badminton shuttle (can be grabbed/rotated/messed with),
   on a **black background**.
2. **Intro screen** — black bg; top badminton (mainly) & table tennis players "pop up" on
   either side as you scroll (parallax cutouts). Mouse leaves a **white ASCII-character trail**.
3. **Transition** — scrolling into the real site flips background to **white**; mouse trail
   becomes a **black ASCII trail**.
4. **Main site** — all FSA content above, modernized with strong design + animation choices.
5. **Nav** — floating **dynamic-island pill** (Home · Members · Support) with Apple
   **liquid-glass** hover/motion feel; condenses/expands as you scroll.
